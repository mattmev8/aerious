A LITTLE ABOUT THE PROJECT

BACK TO
THE THREE-PIECE.

I’m MttRw, a Manchester-based musician with 20+ years of guitar-focussed playing behind me. Aerious started with a simple idea: get back to the basics of a rock band and write songs meant to be played live.

I write and record every part myself, building each song up to demo stage. Now I’m looking for an experienced bassist and drummer to bring these tracks out of the bedroom and onto a stage.
